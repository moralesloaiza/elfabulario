Art Déco illustration for a fable book, in the style of a 1920s–1930s decorative
poster, with influences of linocut printmaking and vintage lithography.

SCENE / ESCENA:
[Describe in 1–3 sentences the key moment of the fable. Name the characters,

STRICT COLOR PALETTE:
- Deep teal (dark blue-green) as the dominant background
- Soft black for secondary backgrounds and outlines
- Gold and mustard ochre for suns, halos, and decorative elements
- Coral and terracotta orange for clothing and warm accents
- Ivory and cream for highlights and skies
- Rust red and sapphire blue as occasional accents
Colors must always be vivid, saturated, and high-contrast.
Never pastel, muted, washed-out, sepia, or melancholic.

TECHNIQUE:
- Flat color fills with minimal shading
- Bold black outlines, firm and confident line work
- Subtle printed-paper grain texture
- Geometrically simplified, stylized shapes
- Recurring motifs: sunbursts, stylized rounded clouds, repeating geometric
  patterns in skies and backgrounds
- No complex gradients, no photorealism, no cartoon or anime style,
  no dramatic chiaroscuro

COMPOSITION:
- Horizontal panoramic format, 3:2 aspect ratio
- Decorative Art Déco border surrounding the entire image, with geometric
  corner ornaments (fans, chevrons, lozenges, stylized suns) in gold on a
  dark ground
- The illustrated scene fills the entire interior up to the inner edge of the border
- A single focal, narrative, symbolic scene — never naturalistic
- Use the extra width to extend the landscape or environment on either side
  of the central action

CHARACTERS:
- Animals recognizable but stylized, expressive without becoming cartoonish
- Human figures simplified, in theatrical poses, dressed in rustic, classical,
  or period clothing as appropriate to the fable

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN:
- Any text, letters, words, numbers, signatures, typography, or calligraphy
  inside the image
- Watermarks or logos
- Disney, Pixar, anime, manga, photorealistic, or 3D styles
- Muted, desaturated, sepia, or monochrome palettes
